About

We built the academy we wished existed.

Cricketers Academy was founded in Dubai by coaches who had seen too many talented young players fail to reach their potential, not because of lack of ability, but because of lack of access to real coaching infrastructure.

Today we are one of the top four cricket academies in the UAE, based in the heart of Dubai.

Our story

How we started.

We opened our first batch in [year] with a handful of players and two coaches. The idea was simple: bring the kind of structured, accountable, curriculum-driven coaching that professional academies use, and make it available to players across Dubai.

Today we train more than 275 players, around 250 boys and 25 girls, from age six to twenty. We field ten age-group squads from Under-10 to Under-19, compete in tournaments right across Dubai, and are now counted among the top four academies in the UAE.

The academy hasn't grown by accident. It's grown because the results speak.

What we believe

Our coaching philosophy.

01

Technique first.

Shortcuts in technique cost players years of frustration later. We take time to build the right foundations, even when it means slowing down.

02

The mental game is not optional.

We coach focus, resilience, and game awareness as deliberately as we coach batting or bowling. The players who last are the ones who can perform under pressure.

03

Progress should be visible.

Parents should be able to see what their child is working on and how they're improving. We communicate, we report, and we hold ourselves accountable.
