Programs

One pathway, from age six to Under-19.

Cricketers Academy is built around age-group cricket. Players start in our Foundation group, move up through ten age-group squads as they develop, and compete in tournaments across Dubai. Our girls' program runs the same coaching and the same pathway.

Ages 6–9 · weekend mornings

Foundation

Where every cricketer starts.

For our youngest players, Foundation is about falling in love with the game and getting the fundamentals right, before competitive age-group cricket begins. Sessions are fun, structured, and built around how six to nine year olds actually learn.

Get this stage right and everything that follows is easier. It is the base the whole pathway is built on.

What's covered

  • First batting, bowling, and fielding skills
  • Hand-eye coordination, balance, and movement
  • Understanding the rules and shape of the game
  • Playing in a group, confidence, and listening
  • A genuine love for cricket, above all

Age-Group Squads

The competitive pathway.

This is the heart of the academy. Ten squads, from Under-10 to Under-19, training and competing in age-group tournaments across Dubai. Players are placed by age and ability, and move up through the age groups as they grow.

Our U10 and U11 squads are among the best in the country, and our U18 and U19 sides are considered one of the top two academy teams in the UAE.

What's covered

  • Advanced batting, bowling, and fielding
  • Match play and tournament preparation
  • Game awareness and cricket IQ
  • Fitness, conditioning, and athleticism
  • Mental performance and handling pressure
  • Video and technical analysis for senior squads

Girls' Cricket

Same coaching. Same pathway.

Cricket is for everyone. Our girls' program gives young female cricketers the same structured coaching, the same facilities, and the same pathway as the boys. It is a close-knit and growing squad of around 25 players.

Whether your daughter is brand new to the game or already playing, there is a place for her here.

What's covered

  • Full batting, bowling, and fielding coaching
  • Dedicated girls-only sessions
  • A pathway into age-group and representative cricket
  • A supportive, encouraging environment
