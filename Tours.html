International tours

The world is our training ground.

Every year, our squads leave Dubai and go where the game lives. Touring sides play competitive cricket abroad, and our players spend time training inside some of the best academies in the world. It changes how they play, and who they are.

India 2022 India 2023 UK 2024 Sri Lanka 2025 Next: 2026
On tour

More than matches. A residency in the game.

A tour is two things at once: a run of competitive cricket against local clubs and academies, and an immersion in how the world's best train, eat, recover, and think.

Competitive matches

Touring squads play a full schedule against local academies and club sides. Different pitches, different conditions, different cricket. Players learn to adapt fast.

Academy residencies

Every year our players spend significant time training inside leading academies around the world, working with their coaches, on their grounds, at their intensity.

Life on tour

Living, travelling, and competing as a team builds the discipline and character that no net session can. Players come home different.

Who gets to go

Earned in the nets, worn on the plane.

Touring squads are selected from our age-group teams, U10 through U19. Selection looks at performance, attitude, and training discipline across the season. If you're putting in the work in Dubai, the world opens up.

  • Selection from our ten age-group squads
  • Coaches travel with every touring side
  • Full match schedule and training programme abroad
  • Families kept informed end to end
